1. Executive Summary
This analysis of 180 raw orders (cleaned to 175 after removing 5 duplicates) shows discounted revenue of ₹97,358.30. COD payment method exhibits a markedly higher return rate (44.4%) than non-COD methods (16.5%), a difference that is statistically significant. The highest-risk segment is COD customers in city-tier 2 with a 54.5% return rate. After excluding quantity outliers, March is the peak revenue month at ₹20,318.90.

2. Key Verified Financial Findings
- Raw discounted revenue (SQL): ₹99,860.20
- Cleaned discounted revenue (Python): ₹97,358.30
- Revenue reduction attributable to duplicate removal: ₹2,501.90
- Customers: 45; Products: 16
- Currency: INR; returns are included in revenue totals.

3. Customer and Payment Behavior
Return rates by standardized payment method:
- COD: 44.4% (24/54)
- Card: 14.7% (10/68)
- UPI: 18.9% (10/53)
COD dominates return volume. Quantity and order_value are strongly correlated (r≈0.98), as expected.

4. Return-Risk Observations
Hypothesis test (one-sided two-proportion z-test):
- H0: COD return rate ≤ non-COD
- H1: COD return rate > non-COD
- z = 3.9318, p = 0.0, risk difference = 27.9 percentage points
- Verdict: H1 is supported at α=0.05
Highest-risk segment: COD × city_tier 2 → 54.5% (12/22).

5. Monthly Revenue Trends
Peak month using all cleaned orders is driven by two extreme quantity outliers (25 and 30 units). After excluding those outliers, the peak shifts to 2026-03 with ₹20,318.90. Outlier correction is an analytical view only; the source data are unchanged.

6. Recommended Business Actions
- Investigate COD fulfilment and return logistics, especially in tier-2 cities (highest observed risk).
- Review the two extreme-quantity orders for data-entry or fraud signals.
- Consider tighter discount controls or pre-authorization for high-quantity COD orders.
- Improve rating capture (15 values were missing) to strengthen product-quality feedback loops.

7. Limitations and Caveats
- 15 ratings were missing and imputed with the median (3.0).
- 12 discount_pct values were missing and filled with 0.
- 5 exact duplicate order submissions were removed.
- Two quantity outliers (25 and 30) heavily inflate January revenue.
- Observational data – statistical associations do not imply causation.
- Sample sizes for some payment × city-tier cells are modest (<30).
- Dataset is a synthetic/educational sample; not official Mamaearth financials.
- No customer lifetime value or repeat-purchase sequence analysis performed.
- Return reasons and product quality signals are absent.
- Payment-method standardization assumes the seven raw labels map only to UPI/Card/COD.
